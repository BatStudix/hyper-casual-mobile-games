Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us

1. Information We Do Not Collect

BAT Studio X does not collect any personal data from our users. We do not require you to create an account, provide your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifiable information to play our games.

2. Advertising

Our games may display advertisements served by third-party ad networks, including Google AdMob. These ad providers may use cookies, device identifiers, and other technologies to deliver personalized ads. Please refer to Google's privacy policy for more information on how they handle data:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Advertising

3. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or rent any personal data with third parties. Since we do not collect personal data, there is no personal information to share.

4. Children's Privacy

Our games are suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any data from children under the age of 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
